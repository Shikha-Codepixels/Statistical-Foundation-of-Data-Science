# Objective
The objective of this practical was to understand and apply basic statistical concepts using the TeachingRatings dataset. The dataset contains rating information for 463 instructors and includes variables such as age, gender, beauty score, evaluation score, number of students, and tenure status.
# Work Performed
The dataset was loaded and analysed using Python and Pandas in Google Colab. First, the structure and dimensions of the dataset were examined to understand the available observations and variables. The nature of the dataset was then identified as cross-sectional data, since the observations represent different instructors/courses rather than repeated measurements over time.

Next, descriptive measures were calculated for the students variable, including the mean, median, minimum, and maximum values. These measures were used to understand the central tendency and range of the number of students participating in the evaluations.
A descriptive statistics table was also generated using Pandas. It included measures such as count, mean, standard deviation, minimum, quartiles, median, and maximum for the numerical variables.
A histogram was created for the beauty variable using Matplotlib to visually examine its distribution. The graph was analysed to understand the concentration and spread of beauty scores.

Finally, the beauty scores were compared by gender. The mean and standard deviation of beauty scores were calculated separately for male and female instructors. The relationship between gender and tenure status was also examined by calculating the percentage of male and female instructors who were tenured.
# Conclusion
This practical provided hands-on experience with basic statistical analysis, descriptive statistics, data visualization, and comparison of groups using a real-world dataset. It helped in understanding how numerical and categorical variables can be analysed and interpreted using Python.
