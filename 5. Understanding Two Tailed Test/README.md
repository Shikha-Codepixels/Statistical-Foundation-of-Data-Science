# Objective

The objective of this practical is to use Python to perform probability calculations and hypothesis testing using the TeachingRatings dataset. The practical focuses on calculating probabilities using the normal distribution and performing a two-tailed Z-test to understand statistical significance.

# What I Did

In this practical, I first loaded the TeachingRatings dataset using Pandas and calculated the mean and standard deviation of the teaching evaluation scores. I then used the normal distribution and Z-scores to calculate the probability of receiving an evaluation score greater than 4.5 and the probability of receiving a score between 3.5 and 4.2.

For the third question, I performed a two-tailed Z-test using a historical mean of 12, standard deviation of 5.5, sample mean of 10.7, and sample size of 36. I calculated the Z-statistic and p-value and compared the results with a 5% significance level.

# Conclusion

The probability calculations showed the likelihood of evaluation scores falling above or within the specified ranges. The two-tailed Z-test resulted in a Z-statistic of approximately -1.42 and a p-value of approximately 0.156. Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, the null hypothesis was not rejected. Therefore, there is insufficient evidence to conclude that the sample mean is significantly different from the historical mean of 12.
